## Political Economy

<ul class="research-list">
<li><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03788">When Does Party Convergence Persist under Alienation-Based Abstention?</a> — arXiv preprint, with Aman Ray. We study when the centripetal force of electoral competition survives even when voters abstain because both parties are too far from their preferences.</li>
<li><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25131">Single-Peakedness Does Not Prevent Leapfrogging under Abstention</a> — arXiv preprint, with Aman Ray. The paper shows that single-peaked preferences alone do not rule out parties reversing their ideological order when abstention affects turnout.</li>
</ul>

## Applied Mathematics and Coding Theory

<ul class="research-list">
<li><strong>A lattice singleton bound</strong> — with B. Sundar Rajan. This work develops a coding-theoretic upper bound for lattice-based code constructions.</li>
<li><strong>On the bounds of certain maximal linear codes in a projective space</strong> — with B. Sundar Rajan. This work studies extremal bounds for linear codes viewed through the geometry of projective spaces.</li>
<li><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33719">A proof of the Braun--Etzion--Vardy bound for binary subspace codes</a> — arXiv preprint; submitted to <em>Finite Fields and Their Applications</em>. The paper proves the Braun--Etzion--Vardy conjecture for binary linear subspace codes.</li>
</ul>

## Miscellaneous

<ul class="research-list">
<li><a href="https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5552758">Patent Valuation under Fragile Institutional Enforcement — A Continuous-Time Markov Approach</a> — SSRN working paper, with Akila Hariharan and Naveen Srinivasan. We model patent value when enforcement is uncertain and evolves as a continuous-time Markov process.</li>
<li><a href="https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5900103">A Critique of Reinforcement-Learning Approaches to Collusion in Oligopoly</a> — SSRN working paper, with Tania Mitra Victoria and Arun Selvan. The paper examines what reinforcement-learning models of collusion can and cannot establish about strategic behaviour in oligopoly.</li>
</ul>
