## Syllabus

- Probability and statistical foundations for machine learning
- Linear regression, least squares, and gradient descent
- Bias--variance trade-off, model selection, and regularisation
- Ridge and Lasso regression
- Logistic regression and classification
- $k$-nearest neighbours and naive Bayes
- Decision trees, random forests, and ensemble methods
- $k$-means clustering and its theoretical foundations
- Support vector machines and kernel methods
- Neural networks and practical model building
